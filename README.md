# Thirukkural Educational AI

## What it does
This application helps you discover the wisdom of Thirukkural through pictures. You upload an image, and the AI finds a relevant Thirukkural (a wise Tamil couplet) that matches what you see in your photo. It shows you the original verse, its meaning in English, the life lesson it teaches, how confident the AI is, and an easy explanation. After reading the explanation, you can ask follow-up questions to learn more about that Thirukkural in a simple, friendly chat.

## How to use it
1. Start the application by running the start command in your project folder
2. Open your web browser to the address shown (usually localhost:3000 or similar)
3. Click to upload an image from your computer (PNG, JPG, or JPEG format, under 5MB)
4. Wait for the analysis to complete
5. Read the Thirukkural, its meaning, life lesson, confidence score, and explanation
6. Ask follow-up questions in the chat area to learn more
7. Click "Back to Upload" to try another image

That's it! No technical knowledge needed - just upload a picture and explore the wisdom it reveals.
